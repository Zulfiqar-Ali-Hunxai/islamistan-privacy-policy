Privacy Policy

Islamistan – Your Islamic Companion

Last Updated: November 26, 2026

Welcome to Islamistan. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Islamistan collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application.

By using Islamistan, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. About Islamistan

Islamistan is an Islamic mobile application designed to provide Islamic resources and services for both Sunni and Shia schools of thought.

The application includes features such as the Holy Quran, prayer times, Qibla direction, Hijri calendar, Islamic books, Hadith collections, Duas, Ziyarat, Tasbih counter, prayer notifications and reminders, and other Islamic educational resources.

2. Information We Collect

Islamistan does not require users to create an account to access its main features. We do not operate our own Firebase or Google Analytics services, and we do not ask you for your name, email address, or phone number in order to use the app.

However, certain application features and third-party services may process information as described in this policy, including location information (Section 3), advertising-related information (Section 7), and notification settings (Section 5).

3. Location Information

Islamistan may request access to your device location (precise or approximate) when you use location-based features.

Location information is used for purposes such as:

  • Calculating accurate daily prayer times.
  • Supporting location-based Islamic services.
  • Helping determine the Qibla direction.

Location permission is requested through your device settings. You can allow or deny this permission at any time. Islamistan requests location access only while you are using the app and does not request background location access.

If you deny location access, some location-based features may not work correctly. Where available, you may use manual location selection instead.

Depending on how a location-based feature works, location information may be processed on your device or transmitted to a relevant service provider to deliver that feature. We do not use device location solely for advertising.

4. App Permissions

Islamistan requests the following Android permissions to provide its features. You can review or change permissions at any time in your device settings.

Permission Why we use it
Internet and network state To load online content, show advertisements, and check whether your device is connected.
Precise and approximate location To calculate prayer times and support the Qibla direction.
Notifications To show prayer time alerts and reminders that you enable.
Run at device startup To restore your scheduled notifications after your device restarts.
Vibration To vibrate your device for notifications and the Tasbih counter, where applicable.
Foreground service (data sync) To complete background tasks, such as updating or syncing content, reliably while the app works.
Advertising ID Used by Google AdMob for advertising delivery and measurement (see Section 7).

5. Notifications and Reminders

Islamistan may send local notifications such as prayer time alerts and reminders. These notifications are scheduled on your device. You can turn notifications on or off at any time in the app or in your device settings.

To keep your reminders working, the app may re-schedule them when your device restarts or when the app is updated.

6. Background Processing

Islamistan may run limited background tasks, such as syncing or updating content, using Android's standard background work and foreground service features. These tasks are used only to support app functionality and are not used to track you.

7. Google AdMob and Advertising

Islamistan uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob is an advertising service provided by Google.

Google AdMob may automatically collect or process certain information through its advertising SDK, including:

  • IP address and approximate location information.
  • Device identifiers and advertising ID.
  • Advertisement interactions and viewing activity.
  • App usage interactions related to advertising.
  • Diagnostic and performance information.

This information may be used for advertising delivery, ad measurement, fraud prevention, and improving advertising services.

Advertising personalization may depend on your device settings, applicable consent requirements, and Google's advertising policies.

For more information about how Google handles information, please visit:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Advertising Policies

8. Firebase and Google Analytics

Islamistan does not currently use Firebase services or Google Analytics for its application services.

This statement does not cover information that may be independently processed by third-party services such as Google AdMob or other service providers used by individual features.

9. How We Use Information

Information processed through the application may be used to:

  • Provide location-based Islamic features.
  • Calculate prayer times based on location.
  • Support the Qibla direction feature.
  • Schedule and deliver prayer notifications and reminders.
  • Display advertisements through Google AdMob.
  • Maintain the functionality and security of the application.
  • Improve the overall user experience.

10. Third-Party Services

Islamistan may use third-party services to provide certain application features, including advertising and location-based services.

These third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

We encourage users to review the privacy policies of relevant third-party service providers.

11. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by third-party services such as Google AdMob, as described in this policy, or disclosed if required by law.

12. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information processed through Islamistan.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

13. Data Retention and Deletion

Islamistan does not maintain a Firebase-based user account database or Google Analytics user database.

Location information and other information processed by third-party services may be retained according to the respective providers' policies and applicable legal requirements.

Settings and preferences stored on your device are removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. You can revoke location and notification permissions through your device settings, and manage advertising preferences through the available Android or Google settings.

If you have questions about information handled by Islamistan, you can contact us using the email address provided below.
